Workshop on Synthesis in LLMs: an in-person workshop at NeurIPS 2026
December 12 or 13, 2026

More information about the workshop, including the call for papers, schedule, and accepted papers will be posted here as it becomes available. Please check back for updates!

This workshop aims to facilitate progress towards measuring, evaluating, applying, and improving the synthesis capabilities of large language models (LLMs) by bringing together researchers to tackle a wide variety of research questions. We encourage submissions on topics including but not limited to:

  • How is synthesis operationalized across different tasks and domains?
  • What applications would benefit from a model that synthesizes well?
  • How well do existing models perform on synthesis tasks and where do they succeed or fail?
  • What methods, training strategies, objectives improve a models ability to synthesize?
  • How should metrics and benchmarks be designed to appropriately measure synthesis outputs?
  • How do models weigh and integrate evidence when synthesizing across sources, and how can we make these more interpretable?

Please direct any questions to synthesisworkshop2026@gmail.com.
